Privacy Policy

How DMA Facility Care handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: August 22, 2026.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we may collect your name, company, phone number, email address, property and service details, preferred contact method, and message.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, prepare a cleaning proposal, provide customer service, and improve our website and services.

Forms, cookies, and service providers

Quote submissions are processed through the form service configured on the website. The site may use essential technical storage and, if added later, analytics or advertising tools. DMA will not add tracking IDs until they are intentionally configured.

Retention and sharing

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed for customer service, business records, and legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to operate the website or respond to your request.

Your choices

You may ask about, update, or request deletion of personal information by emailing info@DMAFacilityCare.ca. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.
